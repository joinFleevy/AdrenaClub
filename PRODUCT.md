# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Athletes & Gym-goers in Algiers (Bab Ezzouar)**: Men looking for high-end strength equipment, cardio, and flexible hours open late until midnight (00:00).
- **Women seeking privacy & quiet**: Looking for a dedicated, respectful fitness sanctuary with 100% female staff and strictly zero music.
- **Combat sport practitioners & kids**: Enrolling in Kickboxing (Coach Abdellah), Olympic Judo (Coach Hmimed), and Wrestling with certified coaching and authentic combat facilities.

## Product Purpose

Adrena Club is the official web portal for a premier 2-level sports complex in Bab Ezzouar, Algiers. It provides immediate, human, and authentic access to live operational status, training schedules, multi-activity membership calculators, and physical gym tours.

## Positioning

A real, visceral, physical Algerian athletic club spanning two dedicated floors (RDC + Sous-Sol) with specialized combat zones (ring, tatami) and an uncompromising 100% zero-music private women's sanctuary. Unlike generic fitness chains, it balances serious athletic training with cultural respect and late-night accessibility.

## Operating Context

- Visitors browse on mobile smartphones while on the go in Algiers or checking schedules before commuting.
- Currency is Algerian Dinar (DZD / DA), payment is cash at the reception desk.
- Cultural bilingualism: French and Arabic spoken and read interchangeably.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Live Schedule Engine**: Real-time evaluation of current session status in Algiers time (`Africa/Algiers`, UTC+1).
- **Dynamic Pricing Calculator**: Instant calculation of multi-activity memberships (Musculation, CrossTraining, Kickboxing, Judo, Lutte).
- **360° Virtual Tour**: WebGL panorama of the physical gym floors.
- **No Online Checkout**: Membership registration and payments happen strictly in-person at the gym desk.

## Brand Commitments

- **Authentic Athletic Human Voice**: Grounded, physical, direct, and respectful. No generic corporate buzzwords or synthetic AI SaaS filler.
- **Bilingual Integrity**: High-fidelity French and Arabic with proper RTL typography and cultural terminology.
- **Tactile, Human Visual World**: Moving away from generic "AI-slop" (black void, synthetic neon gold pills, artificial glassmorphism) toward a grounded, authentic athletic aesthetic.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real coach identities: Coach Abdellah (Kickboxing), Coach Hmimed (Judo).
- Real photographic assets: High-res equirectangular 360° panoramas of RDC and Sous-Sol, coach photos, official club schedules and posters.
- Direct club contact: Bab Ezzouar CUB1, phone `+213 771 57 03 78`.

## Product Principles

1. **Physical & Visceral**: The interface should feel like iron, chalk, leather, and athletic discipline, not an abstract tech SaaS software.
2. **Instant Utility**: Schedules, current live gym status, and transparent prices within 3 seconds of opening the page.
3. **Cultural & Respectful Grounding**: Full respect for the women's zero-music privacy policy and Algerian athletic tradition.
